Potential next steps in our baboon genome project
Dietmar Zinner, Christian Roos, Jeff Rogers

Dataset:
Frozen in May 2026

Questions:
1. How do the newly generated genomes fit into the Papio phylogeny? Updated Papio phylogeny with southern chacmas, Arabian hamadryas, Luangwa valley, and West African olive baboons

2. How to reconcile the seven Y-chromosomal clades and the mitochondrial lineages with the six-species taxonomy of baboons?

Manuscript #1:  An expanded phylogeny for Papio baboons
Analysis and initial draft led by Christian and DPZ

   This paper would be an update of the 2019 paper. I suggest that this would include separate phylogenetic analyses based on nuclear DNA, mtDNA and the Y-chromosome. Results would contrast the three trees and draw conclusions about the historical processes that generated the overall picture. I do not have a clear idea of whether this paper should also include a detailed analysis of admixture and introgression, like Sorensen et al. 2023. We should talk about that. Admixture and introgression might fit here, or be a separate paper?

3. Do we find any indication of gene flow between the Cercocebus-Mandrillus clade and olive baboons? If yes, this could be a reason why in olive baboons the ancestral social system of papionins re-emerged.

Manuscript #2: The origin and diversification of olive baboons
Analysis and initial draft led by ??

    Dietmar and Christian suggested this topic, and I think it would be very interesting and informative. This paper would investigate the possible capture by olive baboons of genes from proto-Guinea baboons, from proto-hamadryas baboons and proto-yellow baboons during the expansion of Papio anubis. Might also include analysis of the possible ghost lineage in East Africa? I think it would be good to also include analysis of the Christian-Dietmar idea about possible introgression from Cercocebus-Mandrillus into P. anubis.

4. How does adaptive evolution in Papio compare to other papionins? dN:dS? Selection scans on orthologues. (A very recent publication, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adv2467)

5. Did western olive baboon pick up already genetic material from (proto-)Guinea baboons? To what degree are they of mixed ancestry? Comparison of western olive baboons with eastern, central and north-eastern olive baboons. (see Koala paper https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adz1430)

6. When did hamadryas baboons reach Arabia? Test of the out-of-Africa hypothesis for baboons based on whole genomes.

7. Do we find different levels of genetic load in local populations of baboons? Guinea baboons, as an ‘end product’ of the pan-African dispersal of baboons, might have been most strongly affected by genetic drift and bottleneck.

8. Targeted analysis of inter-population diversity among baboons from South Africa, Namibia, Gorongosa and Zambia.

Manuscript #3: Detailed analysis of population diversity within and among southern Papio species
Analysis and initial draft led by Kasper and his group (Erik?)

     This paper would present a fine-grained and detailed analysis of inter-population genetic variation among yellows, chacmas and kindas. We would pull in outside data from Gorongosa, etc. I think it makes sense here to present detailed analyses of admixture and introgression. 

9. Investigation of genetic structure across the Awash hybrid zone

Manuscript #4: Genetic structure of the Awash hybrid zone
Analysis and initial draft led by Cliff and Kasper

     An obvious stand-alone paper given that we have whole genome data from >70 Awash baboons.

10. When considering speciation, I always think that it could be interesting to explore which genomic regions drive reproductive isolation among Papio lineages. Are there any patterns of introgression symmetric or directional? How does ILS shape baboon radiation tree?

11. Do we find any indications of clinal diversity in any of the baboon taxa? (see coyote paper https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.73216)

Methods:
1. Checking sequences from museum specimens for possible post-mortem damage (filtering)
2. Checking for close kinship in samples from the same sites (delete closely relates samples from certain analyse)
3. Mapping the new genome sequences to Macaca mulatta (phylogenetic analyses)
4. Mapping the new genome sequences to Papio anubis (population genomic and functional analyses)
5. Update baboon phylogeny including the west and South African samples (West African olive baboons, Cape chacmas, chacmas from Namibia)
	PCA, ML IQ-tree, Admixture, Finestructure, Globetrotter, window-based trees, etc
6. Re-estimate divergence ages; re-estimate Ne and demographic history with updated software
7. Further screening of functional variants.
